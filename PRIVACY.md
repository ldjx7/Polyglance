# Privacy Policy for Polyglance

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Polyglance is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Polyglance handles information when you use our application.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Data Only**: Polyglance operates primarily on your local device. Your settings, local shortcuts, and history records are stored locally on your own machine.
- **No Personal Tracking**: We do not track, collect, or sell your personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical location).

## 2. Translation and Network Services
- When you use online translation providers (e.g. OpenAI, DeepL, Google Translate, Baidu, etc.), the text or image regions you explicitly choose to translate are sent directly to the corresponding third-party API or service endpoint configured by you.
- When using offline translation or OCR models, all processing is performed locally on your device without sending any data over the network.

## 3. Screen and Audio Permissions
- Polyglance requests Screen Recording and Microphone permissions strictly to perform the screenshot, OCR, and screen recording features you explicitly initiate. We do not record or monitor your screen or audio in the background without your command.

## 4. Open Source
Polyglance is open source. You can inspect our source code at: https://github.com/ldjx7/Polyglance

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/ldjx7/Polyglance/issues.
